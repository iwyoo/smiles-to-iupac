# References

smiles-to-iupac implements the substitutive nomenclature rules from:

Favre, H. A.; Powell, W. H. *Nomenclature of Organic Chemistry: Recommendations
and Preferred Names 2013* (the "Blue Book"). Royal Society of Chemistry:
Cambridge, 2013. ISBN 978-0-85404-182-4.
Chapter PDFs: <https://iupac.qmul.ac.uk/BlueBook/PDF/>

Individual rule numbers cited in docstrings (e.g. `P-44.3`) refer to sections of
that book and can be located in the corresponding chapter PDF:

| Chapter | Title                                  | PDF     |
|---------|-----------------------------------------|---------|
| P-1     | General Principles, Rules, and Conventions | `P1.pdf` |
| P-2     | Parent Hydrides                         | `P2.pdf` |
| P-3     | Characteristic (Functional) and Substituent Groups | `P3.pdf` |
| P-4     | Rules for Name Construction              | `P4.pdf` |

No text from the Blue Book is reproduced here beyond short quotations for
citation purposes; consult the chapter PDFs above for the full rules.
